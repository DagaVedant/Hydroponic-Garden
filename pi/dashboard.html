The circulation pump is wired directly to a GFCI outlet with no relay on any GPIO, so no software path can stop it. This is deliberate: switching mains beside an open reservoir to guard a failure mode that does not exist would add risk rather than remove it. The control shows as unavailable rather than being hidden or faked.

Every dose records whether the reading actually moved afterwards. A dose that does not register is treated as a fault and halts dosing rather than retrying, because the cause is an empty bottle, a slipped tube or a dead pump, and none of those are fixed by dosing again.
